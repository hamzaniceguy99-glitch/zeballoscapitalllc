How we work

Private, and plain about it

Zeballos Capital LLC is a limited liability company registered in the State of Florida, with registration details on public record with the Florida Division of Corporations. It is privately funded and buys for its own account.

It is not a fund, a registered investment adviser, a broker-dealer or a licensed real estate brokerage. It manages no money for anyone else, offers no security to anyone, represents no party in a transaction and charges no commission. Nothing on this site is an offer, a solicitation, or investment, legal or tax advice.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Can I invest with you?

No. The company uses its own capital and makes no offer of any security or investment. There is nothing here to buy.

Are you a real estate agency?

No. We're a buyer acting for our own account — not licensed brokers, representing nobody, charging no commission.

What kind of property do you buy?

Income-producing property, including buildings with deferred maintenance or tired tenancies. Tell us what you have and we'll say honestly whether it's a fit.

How quickly can you decide?

A written offer within days of seeing it, because there's no committee and no lender timetable driving the process.

Will you use a broker?

Happily, and we respect a broker's commission where they represent the seller. Plenty of what we see arrives that way.

Do you give investment advice?

No, to anyone, ever. Nothing on this site is advice of any kind.

Considering a sale?

A private conversation and a written offer within days, with no obligation.
